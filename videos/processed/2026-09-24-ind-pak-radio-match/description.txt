Jab India-Pakistan ka match hota tha, toh poore gaon ki dhadkan ek chhote se radio mein band ho jaati thi. 📻🏏

Na TV, na mobile. Bas bargad ka ped, ek purana Murphy radio, aur uske chaaron taraf baithe dadaji, chacha, bachche, sab. Har chauke par taaliyan, har wicket par sannata.

Aur phir wahi hua jiska sabko darr tha: match ke sabse zaroori pal mein radio chup ho gaya! Knob ghumaye, antenna hilaya, par sirf khar-khar. Tabhi gaon ke ek chhote se ladke ne woh jugaad nikala jo har 90s kid jaanta tha: batteries ko haathon mein ragad ke garam karo, wapas daalo, aur... commentary phir se shuru! 😄

Dadaji ne khush hoke chai pilayi, aur jab India jeeta, toh poora gaon phoolon ki baarish mein naach utha. 🇮🇳🎉
